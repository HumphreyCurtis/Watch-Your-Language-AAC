# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 July 2026**

Watch Your Language AAC is designed to work without an account or a developer-operated online service.

## Information the app handles

Phrases, keywords and preferences created in the app are stored locally on
your iPhone and Apple Watch. When the devices are paired, the app uses Apple's
WatchConnectivity framework to keep that content in sync directly between
them.

Speech is generated on the device using Apple's speech synthesis technology.
The developer does not receive the text you enter or speak.

## Information the developer collects

The app does not include:

- developer-operated accounts or servers;
- analytics or tracking;
- advertising;
- third-party analytics or advertising SDKs; or
- purchases or subscriptions.

The app contains links to external websites, including information about
aphasia, the project's research, support and charitable donations. If you
choose to open one, that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Removing your information

You can edit or delete phrases and keywords inside the app. Removing the app
from a device removes the app's locally stored data from that device, subject
to Apple's platform behavior and any device backups you control.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be recorded on this page. For a privacy
question, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/HumphreyCurtis/WatchYourLanguageAAC/issues).
