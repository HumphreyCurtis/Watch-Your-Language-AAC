# Watch Your Language Support

Watch Your Language is listed as **Watch AAC** on the App Store.

## Getting started

1. Add or edit phrases in the iPhone app.
2. Open the app on a paired Apple Watch to receive the latest phrases.
3. Tap a phrase to display it word by word and speak it aloud.
4. Adjust the voice, speech rate and word pace in Settings.

If changes do not appear on the watch, make sure both devices are nearby,
Bluetooth is enabled and the app has been opened on both devices.

## Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HumphreyCurtis/WatchYourLanguageAAC/issues)
for bug reports, accessibility feedback and feature suggestions. Please
describe the device models, OS versions and what you expected to happen. Do
not include private health information or phrases you do not want to share
publicly.

## Important limitation

Watch Your Language is a communication aid, not a medical device or emergency
service. Do not rely on it as the only way to request urgent assistance.
Contact local emergency services when immediate help is needed.
